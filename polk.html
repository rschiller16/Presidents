James Knox Polk

James Knox Polk was the 11th president of the United States, serving from 1845 to 1849.

Before becoming president, Polk served as the 13th Speaker of the House of Representatives and the ninth governor of Tennessee.

A protégé of Andrew Jackson, Polk was a member of the Democratic Party and an advocate of Jacksonian democracy.
