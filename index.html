Presidents Through the Centuries

The office of President of the United States has been held by many different leaders throughout American history.

George Washington became the first president of the United States in 1789 and helped establish many traditions for future presidents.

James Knox Polk served during the 1800s, John F. Kennedy served during the 1900s, and Barack Obama became president during the 2000s.
