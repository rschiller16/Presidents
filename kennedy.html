John F. Kennedy

John F. Kennedy was the 35th President of the United States from 1961 to 1963 and was the youngest man elected to the office.

On November 22, 1963, when he was hardly past his first thousand days in office, Kennedy was assassinated in Dallas, Texas.

President Kennedy's administration became associated with the term Camelot. He was followed in office by Lyndon B. Johnson.
